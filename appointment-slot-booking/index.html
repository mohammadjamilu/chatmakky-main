Getting started

Set up in three steps

Open the Calendar Booking tool from your ChatMakky dashboard and follow these steps.

Pick your calendar

Choose Google Calendar or a native calendar. Connect Google in one click; native calendars need no Google account.

Set availability and staff

Set working hours for each weekday with simple AM/PM time pickers, add staff, choose which calendars to check for conflicts, then save.

Share your link

Publish your booking page and share the link. New bookings show up under Recent Bookings and the Upcoming tab.

Full control

Automation

Turn every booking into a workflow

A booking is only the start. Collect the details you need, trigger your flows and get paid.

Custom questions

Add extra fields between Email and Notes, like a WhatsApp number or a Facebook page. Mark each as required or optional.

{{booking.whatsapp}}{{booking.facebook}}

Every answer is available in your flow as {{booking.key}}.

Trigger a flow after booking

Start a ChatMakky flow the moment someone books: send a WhatsApp or Messenger message, tag the contact, notify your team.

Conversion tracking

Track bookings as conversions in your ad and analytics pixels, so you know which campaigns bring appointments.

Paid bookings (Stripe)

Charge for consultations or sessions by asking for payment at booking time with Stripe.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a Google account?

No. Connect Google Calendar if you use it, or choose a native calendar that works without any Google account.

Can I collect extra information from bookers?

Yes. Add custom questions to the booking form and use the answers in your flows as {{booking.key}}.

Can I charge for appointments?

Yes, you can turn on paid bookings with Stripe.

Can I have more than one team member?

Yes. Add staff, assign bookings and set availability per person.

Where do I see my bookings?

Under Recent Bookings and the Upcoming tab inside the Calendar Booking tool, plus on your connected calendar.
