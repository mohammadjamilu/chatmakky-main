Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ChatMakky ("ChatMakky", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use our website, dashboard, and services that let businesses build and deploy AI agents across messaging channels such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, TikTok, and web chat widgets (collectively, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • ✓ Account information — name, email address, password, company details, and billing information you provide when you sign up.
  • ✓ Channel connection data — tokens, page IDs, and permissions granted when you connect third-party channels (e.g. WhatsApp Business, Meta, TikTok, Google) to your ChatMakky agent.
  • ✓ Conversation data — messages, attachments, and metadata exchanged between your customers and your AI agent through connected channels, which you control and which we process on your behalf.
  • ✓ Usage data — log data, device/browser information, IP address, and interactions with the dashboard, collected automatically.
  • ✓ Payment data — processed by our payment provider (e.g. Stripe); we do not store full card numbers on our servers.

2. How We Use Information

  • ✓ To provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including running your AI agents across connected channels.
  • ✓ To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • ✓ To communicate with you about updates, security alerts, and support.
  • ✓ To monitor usage, detect abuse, and improve reliability and performance.
  • ✓ To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. AI Processing & Third-Party Providers

Where you connect your own AI provider keys (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or others) or use channel APIs (Meta, TikTok, Twilio, Google), your data may be processed by those third parties in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms. ChatMakky acts as an intermediary that routes messages and data to the providers and channels you configure; we do not sell conversation content to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • ✓ Service providers who help us operate the Service (hosting, analytics, payments, customer support).
  • ✓ Third-party channels and AI providers you explicitly connect to your account.
  • ✓ Authorities, when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of ChatMakky, our users, or the public.
  • ✓ A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and conversation data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You may request deletion of your data as described in Section 7.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and understand how the Service is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling some cookies may affect functionality.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. International Transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own. We take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers where required by law.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be indicated by updating the "Last updated" date above, and, where appropriate, additional notice will be provided.
