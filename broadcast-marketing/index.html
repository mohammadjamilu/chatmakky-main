Choosing a channel

Match the channel to your goal

Our recommendations, based on how each channel works. Test them with your own audience.

Time-sensitive alerts

Flash sales, last-day reminders and delivery updates need to be seen quickly and kept short.

Best fit: SMS, Push, WhatsApp

Detailed offers and newsletters

Longer content with images, links and a clear layout, where you also want to measure clicks.

Best fit: Email

Follow-ups in an ongoing chat

When a customer has already messaged you, replying in the same app feels natural.

Best fit: WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram

High-touch outreach

Confirmations, reminders and conversations that benefit from a human-like voice.

Best fit: Phone (AI voice)

Community updates

Announcements for people who already follow you in community-style apps.

Best fit: Telegram, Discord

Staying in touch beyond 24 hours

Use channel features designed for later replies, such as HUMAN_AGENT or approved templates.

Best fit: Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp

Day 1, later

Check each channel’s analytics afterwards and adjust timing and wording for the next campaign.

Stay compliant

Rules that protect your reach

Platforms such as Meta set strict messaging rules. The Broadcast Hub shows the relevant limits right where you send.

24-hour messaging window

On Messenger, standard broadcasts go only to contacts who messaged you in the last 24 hours. Instagram shows the same status for information.

Approved templates for WhatsApp

WhatsApp broadcasts use Meta-approved templates synced from your Business account, so content must be approved before you send.

HUMAN_AGENT replies

For replies up to 7 days after a person’s last message on Messenger and Instagram, Meta offers the HUMAN_AGENT tag.

Opt-outs and consent

Keep an unsubscribed list for SMS, message only people who have contacted you or agreed to hear from you, and follow local regulations.

Getting started

From setup to send in three steps

Open the Broadcast Hub, expand a channel and click its Open button.

Connect the channel

Link the account in your AI Agent’s Channels tab, such as a Telegram bot token, a Discord bot, a Facebook Page, WhatsApp Business or SMTP for email.

Choose your audience

Select contacts, segment by tag or agent, or target widget users, depending on the channel.

Compose, schedule and track

Write or generate your message, send it now or later, then follow delivery, opens and clicks where the channel supports them.
