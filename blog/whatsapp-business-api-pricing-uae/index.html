WhatsApp Business API Pricing in the UAE, Explained in Plain AED

The free WhatsApp Business app and the WhatsApp Business Platform, the API, are two different products. If you want an AI agent, a shared team inbox or proper broadcasts, you need the API. Here is exactly what it costs in the UAE.

First: the app is not the API

  • The app is free. It runs on one phone, for one person. No AI agents, no real automation, no team inbox.
  • The API is built for teams and software. This is what AI agents, CRMs and broadcast tools connect to.

How Meta charges now

Since 1 July 2025, Meta charges per delivered message, priced by the message category and the country of the person receiving it. These are Meta's UAE rates from the rate card effective October 2026. Meta reviews rates each quarter, so check its pricing page before you set a yearly budget.

Message typeUsed forUAE rate per delivered message

Messages customers send to you are always free. After a customer messages you, a 24 hour service window opens. Reply inside it and your messages count as cheap service messages. Outside the window, you must send an approved template message, charged by its category. Customers who arrive from a click-to-WhatsApp ad open a free entry window, where follow-ups cost nothing for a limited period.

A worked example

A Dubai business sends 1,000 marketing messages and 2,000 utility messages in a month.

  • Marketing: 1,000 x AED 0.2115 = AED 211.50
  • Utility: 2,000 x AED 0.0576 = AED 115.20
  • Total Meta fees: AED 326.70 for the month

On top of Meta fees you pay for the platform you use. Basic inbox tools start around AED 150 a month. Full AI platforms run higher. And if a team sets everything up for you, there is a setup fee, which should cover number connection, Meta verification, template approval and AI training.

Five ways to keep the bill small

  1. Use the right category. A booking confirmation that hides an offer inside is billed as marketing. Keep utility messages clean.
  2. Reply inside the 24 hour window. It is the cheapest conversation you will ever have.
  3. Get opt-in before broadcasts. Blocked numbers waste money and damage your quality rating.
  4. Watch your quality rating in WhatsApp Manager. A poor rating can limit how many people you can message.
  5. Answer fast. An AI agent that replies in seconds finishes more conversations inside the free service allowance.

Questions owners ask us

Can I use my existing number on the API?

Usually yes. Your existing business number can be connected in most cases.

Does Meta charge for messages customers send me?

No. Incoming messages are free. You pay for delivered template messages and for service replies beyond the free monthly allowance.

Why did UAE prices change in October 2026?

Meta raised the UAE marketing rate on 1 October 2026 and started charging service replies beyond the first 1,000 per month. Rates are reviewed quarterly.
